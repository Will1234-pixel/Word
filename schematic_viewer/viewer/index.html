How to use

  • Click a cross-reference such as 052.225, a signal label (3A), a tag, a cable number or a terminal: the viewer opens the destination page, zooms in and flashes the match.
  • What this is: every click also shows a small note next to the cursor saying what the clicked text is (read from the drawing). Press More, or look at the top of the Selection tab, for the full explanation: service, loop type, cable, module, the signals it produces and the other sheets it appears on.
  • Click a wire to highlight the whole conductor on this page, read what it carries and list where it goes. Moving the mouse over a wire previews that conductor in blue. Conductors are joined at corners and junction dots only; a wire that ends on the middle of another line without a dot is not treated as a branch.
  • Explain sheet (toolbar button, key E, or Explain next to a sheet in the Sheets tab): a summary of the sheet, the facts read from it and, on functional logic diagrams, one row per output. Click a row to highlight all its wiring and see the logic as an outline; click a line of the outline to highlight that input. Follow to sheet walks a continuation signal to the next sheet and opens its logic there; the hops are added to the Trail.
  • Back returns to the page and location you came from (also Backspace).
  • Several matches: a list appears; choose the right one.
  • Status badges: verified label found and the destination points back; matched label found, no back pointer; back pointer destination points back but the label differs; same label repeated label, not a verified connection; ambiguous/unresolved needs your judgement; signal path a hop made with Follow to sheet.
  • Mouse: wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click zooms in. Keys: F fit, E explain sheet, +/- zoom, arrows pan, Esc closes notes and clears highlights.
  • Manual links: select a text, press + Link, then click the destination (any page). Use Confirm / Reject on automatic links.

Every explanation is built only from text and geometry on the drawings. Where the drawing leaves something open the note says so, for example "unlabelled wire" or "no signal name written". Nothing about plant behaviour is inferred.
