How to use

  • Click a cross-reference such as 052.225, a signal label (3A), a tag, a cable number or a terminal: the viewer opens the destination page, zooms in and flashes the match.
  • Click a wire to highlight the whole conductor on this page and list where it goes.
  • Back returns to the page and location you came from (also Backspace).
  • Several matches: a list appears; choose the right one.
  • Status badges: verified label found and the destination points back; matched label found, no back pointer; back pointer destination points back but the label differs; same label repeated label, not a verified connection; ambiguous/unresolved needs your judgement.
  • Mouse: wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click zooms in. Keys: F fit, +/- zoom, arrows pan.
  • Manual links: select a text, press + Link, then click the destination (any page). Use Confirm / Reject on automatic links.
